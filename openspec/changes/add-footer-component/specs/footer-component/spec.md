## Requirements

- The application shall render a footer section at the bottom of the page.
- The footer shall display the text "Design and developed by Mugilan S".
- The footer shall appear in both authenticated and unauthenticated app states.
- The footer shall use the existing visual theme without interrupting the primary content flow.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A footer is visible at the bottom of the page after the main content renders.
2. The footer text matches the requested attribution exactly.
3. The footer remains present when the user switches between the login view and the dashboard view.
